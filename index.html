Questions & Answers

Do I need an account to track my order?

No. Customers only need the order number or company name. Accounts are for shop admins.

Where do I find my order number?

It is on your receipt or invoice and looks like SO-001.

What do the three statuses mean?

Accepted: the shop has your order. Delivering: it is on the way. Shipped: it was delivered.

My order is past its deadline. What now?

Contact the shop with your order number. Late orders are highlighted for the admin too.

Can other people see my details?

The public search shows only order number, customer, amount, deadline and status. Passwords are hashed and all traffic is encrypted.
